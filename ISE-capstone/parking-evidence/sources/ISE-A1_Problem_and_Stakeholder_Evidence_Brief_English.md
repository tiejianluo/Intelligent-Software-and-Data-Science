# ISE-A1 | Problem and Stakeholder Evidence Brief

- Course：Intelligent Software Engineering
- Release：2026-09-21 16:30 (Asia/Shanghai)
- Deadline：2026-09-28 16:30 (Asia/Shanghai)
- Maximum：5
- Platform：https://learn.spaiq.ai

## Objective

Show that a problem in an authentic workflow merits a semester-sized software intervention.

## Required work

1. Investigate an authentic workflow using at least two independent source types.
2. Identify users, affected parties, decision makers, constraints, and current responsibility.
3. Quantify pain, baseline, value measure, and failure cost.
4. Compare a software intervention with at least one non-software alternative.
5. Bound semester scope, non-goals, assumptions, and stop conditions.

## Deliverables

- Evidence ledger
- Problem/value statement
- Stakeholder/workflow map
- Normal, boundary, and abuse scenarios
- AI-use and source-verification log

## Platform submission

In the matching learn.spaiq.ai task, submit a 150–300 word result summary and a stable GitHub repository URL with commit/tag. Keep notebook, PDF, source, raw results, and AI-use log in that versioned repository. Screenshots or demos do not replace code, provenance, or reproduction instructions.

## Common requirements

- Executable top-to-bottom; freeze seeds, dependencies, data version, and environment.
- Retain failed runs, negative results, and evidence that challenges the claim.
- Disclose AI tool/task, accepted and rejected advice, and independent verification.
- Do not fabricate data, sources, experiments, users, execution, or results; never expose credentials or personal data.

## Five-point rubric

- Problem, context, and method choice: 1.25
- Execution, artifact quality, and reproducibility: 1.50
- Analysis, counterexample, and limits: 1.25
- Communication, reflection, and AI verification: 1.00

## Deadline and help boundary

The submission window is exactly seven days and platform timestamps are authoritative. Reopening requires teacher-recorded permission. The teacher and AI Tutor may clarify goals, identify evidence gaps, suggest methods, or show a small example from another task; they will not disclose a complete solution to this assessed task.
